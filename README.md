# 🛍️Retail_Sales_Data_Agent  

📌 **Project Overview**

The Retail Sales Agent project focuses on analysing retail sales data to understand customer purchasing behaviour,  
sales performance, product performance, and overall business trends. The project demonstrates how data analytics, SQL,  
data cleaning, visualization, and business intelligence can be used to transform raw retail data into meaningful insights  
that support better business decisions.

🎯 **Project Objectives**

**The main objectives of this project are to:**  

Analyse retail sales performance.  
Identify high-performing and low-performing products.  
Analyse sales trends over time.  
Understand customer purchasing patterns.  
Calculate important sales metrics.  
Identify factors affecting sales performance.  
Create dashboards to communicate insights.  
Provide data-driven recommendations for improving retail performance.  

📊 **Key Business Questions**  

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

What are the total sales and total quantity sold?  
What is the average sales value per transaction?  
Which products generate the highest sales?  
Which products have the lowest sales?  
Which categories perform best?  
Which stores or locations generate the most revenue?  
How do sales change by day, month, and year?  
What are the busiest sales periods?  
What products are most frequently purchased?  
Who are the most valuable customers?

💻 **Example SQL Analysis**  
Total Sales  
SELECT  
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_sales  
FROM retail_sales;  
Sales by Product Category  
SELECT  
    product_category,  
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_sales  
FROM retail_sales  
GROUP BY product_category  
ORDER BY total_sales DESC;  
Monthly Sales  
SELECT  
    YEAR(transaction_date) AS year,  
    MONTH(transaction_date) AS month,  
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_sales  
FROM retail_sales  
GROUP BY  
    YEAR(transaction_date),  
    MONTH(transaction_date)  
ORDER BY  
    year,  
    month;  
Top Products  
SELECT  
    product_id,  
    ROUND(SUM(transaction_qty * unit_price), 2) AS total_sales  
FROM retail_sales  
GROUP BY product_id  
ORDER BY total_sales DESC  
LIMIT 10;
